## What works on this account (from the last 15 posts)

- **Reels with a direct ask outperform everything.** Coffee table trunk reel
  with "DM us for prices and details": 71 likes, 24 comments, best on the
  account. Hand-painted bar cabinet ("DM us to bring this home"): 38 / 17.
  Chest of drawers ("People spend lakhs designing a beautiful home..."):
  28 / 16. Elvia chest ("DM ELVIA"): 47 / 6.
- **Image posts with hashtag dumps and emoji got 10 to 14 likes and 0
  comments.** Stop making them.
- **A problem hook beats a description hook.** "People spend lakhs designing a
  beautiful home... and then ruin it with boring storage furniture" pulled
  comments. "A chair that feels like home ✨" did not.
- **Function reveals land.** Trunk opens into a bar, chest unfolds into a
  vanity, cabinet "opens like wings". Show it closed, then open.

## My positions

1. Storage is the most neglected piece in an expensive home. People spend lakhs
   on the room and then buy the chest of drawers last, from a catalogue.
2. One real piece beats a room of matching ones. Buy fewer things, made by
   hand, and let one of them carry the room.
3. Made to order is a feature, not a delay. The wait is how you get your size,
   your leather, your kilim.
4. A rug is the first thing to choose in a room, not the last. Pick it, then
   build around it.
5. Indian craft does not need to be dressed up as "boho" or "ethnic" to be
   modern. A kilim on a clean frame is already modern.
